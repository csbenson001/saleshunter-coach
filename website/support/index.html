Common questions

Recording won't start

Sign in to SalesHunter Coach first, then allow the microphone in iPhone Settings → SalesHunter Coach. SalesHunter Coach records the room through the microphone only. It cannot record phone calls, FaceTime, or audio from other apps — iOS provides no API for that to any third-party app.

Does a failed sync lose the recording?

No. A finished recording is held in an on-device queue and is only removed once it has synced. Open the app when you are back online, or retry from Settings → Sync.

Browsing and moving recordings

Open Library and pick the personal or organization scope. Press and hold a recording to move it into a folder, share a copy to an organization, move it to an organization, or delete it.

Setting up the voice keyboard, and why it needs Full Access

Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → SalesHunter Coach Voice, then open it and turn on Allow Full Access. A keyboard extension cannot record audio, so Full Access is what lets it hand the work to the SalesHunter Coach app and get the transcript back. The keyboard itself never opens a network connection and never records or stores what you type. Without Full Access it still works as a basic key row.
