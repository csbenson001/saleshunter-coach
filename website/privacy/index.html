Privacy

You should know exactly where your meetings go.

This policy explains how the hosted version of SalesHunter Coach handles accounts, recordings, transcripts, and AI usage data. The open-source desktop build, with cloud services disabled, runs entirely on your machine and the third-party providers you configure.

Last updated: July 27, 2026

The short version: SalesHunter Coach captures microphone audio only after you press record. Audio and transcripts sync to your SalesHunter Coach account so you can review them across devices. You can permanently delete your account and its data from inside the app, or request deletion without installing the app.

Data we collect

Account dataName, email address, sign-in method, and sessions, used to create and secure your account.
Meeting contentAudio you actively record, plus transcripts, titles, summaries, folders, and organization assignment.
Service usageTranscription seconds and AI credits consumed, used to apply plan limits and prevent abuse.
Technical dataBasic request, error, and device/browser information needed for security and service operation.

How we use data

  • To provide live transcription, your recording library, cross-device sync, and the personal or organization folders you choose.
  • To send audio to a transcription service on your behalf, and to a model provider when you use AI features.
  • To keep accounts secure, calculate usage against plan limits, and diagnose service failures.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your meeting content for advertising.

Storage and third parties

The hosted version stores account data, recording metadata, and encrypted-in-transit audio objects with our cloud storage provider. Realtime transcription and AI features are performed by third-party model providers. If you sign in with Google or Apple, that provider handles sign-in data under its own policy. These providers process data only to deliver the SalesHunter Coach service.

Retention and deletion

Your account data and personal recordings are kept until you delete them. Deleting your account permanently removes the account, the audio and transcripts of your personal recordings, folders, and usage data, and cannot be undone. On iOS: Settings → Account → Delete account. On Android: the Account panel → Delete account. If you cannot open the app, follow the account deletion page to request removal by email from your registered address. If you own a shared organization, you must transfer or delete it first, so that other members' shared data is not removed by accident.

A finished recording that fails to sync is held in the app's support storage on your device until it uploads successfully, then removed automatically. Deleting the app clears that pending data from the device.

Your responsibilities and choices

Obtain consent from every participant before recording, as required by the laws that apply to you. The SalesHunter Coach iOS app cannot capture phone calls or system audio from other apps; it uses the microphone only when you actively start a recording.

Contact us

For privacy questions, data requests, or feedback on this policy, email support@saleshunterlive.com.
