Account deletion

Deleting your account really deletes it.

This page explains how to permanently delete your SalesHunter Coach account and every recording synced to it. You can do it yourself in the app, or email us if you cannot open the app.

What deletion removes: the account itself, and every recording synced to it — audio files, transcripts, summaries, folders, and usage history. Deletion is permanent and irreversible; there is no backup to restore from.

Delete in the app

  • iOSOpen SalesHunter Coach → Settings → Account → Delete account, then confirm with "Delete my account and data".
  • AndroidOpen SalesHunter Coach → Account (top right) → Delete account, then confirm with "Delete my account and data".

Both platforms show a confirmation dialog describing the scope, and require a second tap before anything is removed. Afterwards the app returns to the sign-in screen, and any recordings still waiting to upload on that device are cleared too.

If you own a shared organization

Deletion is blocked while you still own a shared organization. That is deliberate: removing the workspace outright would take other members' shared recordings with it. Transfer the organization to another member, or delete it, from the desktop app first — then delete your account.

Deleting without the app installed

You do not need to install or reinstall SalesHunter Coach. Email support@saleshunterlive.com from the address your account is registered to, with the subject "SalesHunter Coach account deletion" and the account email in the message. We confirm your identity through that address and then delete the account, with exactly the same scope as in-app deletion.
