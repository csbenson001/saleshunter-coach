# SalesHunter Coach

SalesHunter Coach is a real-time sales-call coaching product. This glossary names the live coaching concepts that distinguish it from post-call intelligence and CRM workflow products.

## Language

**Competitive mention**:
A final transcript utterance that matches a competitor's product-name heuristic and triggers competitor-specific coaching.
_Avoid_: Competitor alert

**Micro-kill-sheet**:
A four-line competitive coaching card containing a trigger, buyer trap, counter-strike, and pivot question in no more than 280 characters.
_Avoid_: Long-form battlecard, competitor brief

**Buyer trap**:
The mistaken equivalence or assumption behind the buyer's competitor claim.
_Avoid_: Objection

**Counter-strike**:
The exact short response a representative can say immediately after a competitive mention.
_Avoid_: Differentiation notes, talking points

**Pivot question**:
The qualifying question that follows a counter-strike and tests whether the incumbent solves the buyer's live-call problem.
_Avoid_: Follow-up
