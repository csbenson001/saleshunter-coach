# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Quota-carrying sales representatives and revenue leaders use SalesHunter Coach during and after high-stakes sales calls. During a live call, the primary job is to understand the buyer's move and choose the next useful question without losing conversational flow. Afterward, users review commitments and missed moments to improve the next call.

## Product Purpose

SalesHunter Coach turns live meeting audio into timely, commercially useful coaching. It exists to help representatives handle objections, protect deal momentum, and improve win rates while action can still change the outcome. Success means a rep can absorb a cue in seconds, use it naturally, and move the buyer toward a concrete commercial next step.

## Brand Personality

Direct, calm, and commercially sharp. The product should feel like an experienced sales coach at the rep's shoulder: confident without swagger, candid without aggression, and precise under pressure.

## Anti-references

- Post-call note takers that report what happened only after the deal can no longer be influenced.
- Dense dashboards, walls of generated text, and decorative alerts that compete with the live conversation.
- Game-like sales theater, noisy urgency, or combative competitor language that weakens buyer trust.
- Unfamiliar controls or gratuitous motion that make a live-call workflow harder to scan and operate.

## Design Principles

- Act while the moment is still recoverable.
- Give the rep the next question, not a lecture.
- Preserve buyer trust while maintaining commercial spine.
- Make evidence and deal progress visible, not performative.
- Keep sensitive conversation data local and user-controlled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard operation, focus visibility, labels, and status announcements. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Never make color the only carrier of meaning, and keep live cues readable under time pressure and at compact window sizes.
